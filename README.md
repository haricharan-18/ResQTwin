# 🚨 ResQTwin

## AI-Powered Disaster Management & Digital Twin for Intelligent Evacuation

ResQTwin is an offline-first disaster management and intelligent evacuation platform that combines a **Digital Twin, disaster simulation, risk intelligence, crowd simulation, evacuation routing, rescue coordination, shelter allocation, and an AI-style emergency assistant** into a single command center.

The system is designed to help emergency teams understand **what is happening, where the risk is increasing, how people are moving, which routes are safe, and where resources should be deployed**.

---

## 🎯 Problem

During a disaster, emergency teams need to make decisions quickly while dealing with:

- Rapidly changing disaster conditions
- Blocked roads and exits
- Large crowds moving through limited routes
- High-risk buildings and zones
- Rescue requirements
- Limited shelter and medical capacity
- Increasing congestion during evacuation
- Poor connectivity or internet availability

Traditional dashboards often show isolated information.

**ResQTwin connects these decisions into one live disaster intelligence system.**
